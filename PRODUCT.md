## Platform

web

## Users

Repositioned target (per client instruction): performance-driven professionals and athletes in and around Frankfurt am Main who want 1:1 physiotherapy on their own schedule and are willing to pay for it directly, rather than working within statutory-insurance (GKV) queues and 20-minute slots. Secondary: existing patients returning for rehab/regeneration follow-ups.

## Product Purpose

A private physiotherapy practice's marketing site: reposition an already-private-pay practice (see Evidence) as a premium, performance-oriented service for people who can and will pay for better, less-compromised care — and convert visits into appointment requests.

## Positioning

Not a Kassenpraxis. The practice already operates on private prescription (privatärztliche Verordnung) or self-pay (Selbstzahler) terms with VAT charged on services — evidence it sits outside the statutory-insurance system already. The redesign's job is to make that positioning explicit and desirable instead of leaving it implicit/uncommunicated: full-length 1:1 sessions with the same therapist, modern regeneration tech (near-infrared, compression therapy), and scheduling that fits a demanding career — not a discount-clinic queue.

## Product Principles

1. State the private-pay positioning outright — don't leave visitors to infer why there's no Krankenkasse billing.
2. Every section should read as "less compromise, more result" — full session time, same therapist, modern equipment, flexible scheduling — not generic clinical reassurance.
3. Booking/contact stays one click away; this is a conversion-first Persuade-mode site, same as the practice's own goal of filling a premium calendar.
4. Tone is confident and performance-driven, not clinical-cold or discount-friendly — copy should read like a service chosen deliberately, not defaulted into.
